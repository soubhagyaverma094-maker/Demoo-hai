The Central Idea

Different rivers, climbing down from different mountains, flowing toward a single ocean.

The perennial idea at the heart of this project is simple: that beneath the vast diversity of the world's religions lies a shared human longing — to understand our origin, to live rightly, and to connect with something greater than ourselves.

Each tradition answers these questions in its own language, shaped by its people, land, and age. Seen side by side, their differences are real and worth honouring — and so are their striking echoes: compassion, truth, service, and the dignity of every human being.

A note on framing. This site presents a philosophical and spiritual interpretation — the idea of "one truth behind many paths." It is offered as a lens for reflection, not as historical fact or as a statement that all religions are identical. Historical dates and claims are clearly separated from belief throughout.

Reading Honestly

Documented History & Traditional Belief

Good faith means telling the two apart. Here is how historians and believers each describe the same traditions — both worth understanding, kept clearly distinct.

📜 What the Record Shows

Historically documented
  • The Rigveda was composed c. 1500–1200 BCE and transmitted orally for centuries before being written.
  • Siddhartha Gautama is placed by most historians in the 5th–4th century BCE in the Gangetic plain.
  • The historical Jesus is widely accepted to have lived in 1st-century Roman Judea.
  • Guru Nanak (1469–1539 CE) founded Sikhism; his life is well documented.

✦ What Traditions Hold

Traditional belief
  • Hindu tradition regards the Vedas as eternal (apaurusheya), heard by sages rather than authored.
  • Buddhists hold that the Buddha attained full enlightenment beneath the Bodhi tree.
  • Christians hold that Jesus is the Son of God, resurrected after crucifixion.
  • Sikhs hold that Guru Nanak received divine revelation calling all people to one God.
Why This Exists

One Truth · One Humanity · One Creator

Three ideas hold this project together — offered as reflection, not doctrine.

☀

One Truth

The conviction that a single ultimate reality lies behind the many names given to it — approached, never fully captured, by any one tradition.

🤝

One Humanity

That the people of every faith share one origin and one home. Difference is real, but it need not divide; understanding is the beginning of peace.

✦

One Creator

The intuition, expressed differently in each tradition, that existence flows from a single source — the ground of being that religions call by a thousand names.
